About

The short version.

The same five stages behind every build here — Gather, Clean, Model, Evaluate, Ship — plus the four areas I'm exploring now.

I work as a full-stack data scientist — the kind who stays with a problem after the model runs, until it is somewhere a real person can open it.

So most of what is on this page started as a messy public dataset and ended as a deployed app. Along the way I keep the notebooks teaching-style, because a result nobody can follow is not finished.

That habit is what got me recognised on Kaggle as a Notebook Expert, and it is why I lean toward work that is visually satisfying as well as technically sound.

Currently exploring LLM fine-tuning Diffusion models Vector databases MLOps
